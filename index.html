About Verdant

An organic plant nursery built around one idea: plant ownership should feel simple, sustainable and genuinely supported.

Our story

Grown from a simple frustration

Verdant began as an organic plant nursery for people who love the idea of a greener home but keep losing plants to unclear advice. Too often, a beautiful plant arrives with a single care card — and then the questions start: how much light, how often to water, why are the leaves turning yellow?

So we built something different. Verdant pairs nursery-grown plants and organic gardening essentials with a plant recommender that matches plants to real rooms and real routines, plus care guides and a personal dashboard that keeps track of watering and fertilising.

This website is a demonstration store built with HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, with a Node.js backend. It shows how an organic nursery could support customers end to end — from choosing a plant to keeping it thriving. Orders, messages and newsletter sign-ups are saved on the server for the nursery administrator to review.

Quick answers

How do I choose my first plant?

Start with the plant recommender. It considers your light, watering habits and experience level, then ranks plants by compatibility.

Are the plants safe for pets?

Pet safety depends on the species and the exposure. Every product page lists known toxicity information, and the recommender excludes plants known to be toxic to your selected pets. Always verify before bringing a plant home.

Is checkout real?

No — checkout is a demonstration. No payment is taken and no order is shipped, but your order details are saved on the Verdant server so the nursery administrator can review them.

Where is my data stored?

Cart, wishlist, questionnaire answers, saved plants and form submissions are stored in your browser’s LocalStorage. Clearing site data removes them.
